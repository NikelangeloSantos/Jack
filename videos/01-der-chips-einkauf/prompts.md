## Falls ein Clip fehlerhaft ist

Nur dann in `thema.toml` bei der Szene `korrektur = ["name"]` ergänzen und neu bauen. Verfügbar:

- `ohren`: His ears stay soft, floppy and hanging down the whole time.
- `hande`: His hands keep their furry human shape with five fingers.
- `schuhe`: He keeps his white sneakers on, never barefoot.
- `schwanz`: His long fluffy tail stays visible and attached.
- `kein_text`: All packaging is plain and unlabeled.
- `kein_auftauchen`: Nothing appears out of nowhere; objects only move when someone moves them.
